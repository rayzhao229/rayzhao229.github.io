Privacy Policy

How Minghua Ruiyi collects, uses and protects information from visitors to this website.

1. What we collect

When you contact us through the enquiry form, by email or WhatsApp, we may collect: your name, company name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number, and the purchase details you provide (category, quantity, specification, destination country and similar).

As you browse this site, Google Analytics is loaded to measure traffic and page views. Only anonymised usage data is collected; IP addresses are handled under Google's own policy.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to: reply to your enquiry and provide a quotation and supply plan; arrange preparation, inspection and shipment; and, where you have agreed, send you relevant commercial information about our products. We do not sell your information to third parties unless required by law or necessary to complete a transaction.

3. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for the duration of our business relationship and for a reasonable period afterwards to handle orders, after-sales matters and follow-up. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

4. Cross-border transfers

We are based in China, so information you submit is transferred to and stored on our servers and with our partners. If data protection rules apply in your region — for example the EU GDPR or Saudi / UAE local regulations — we can provide the necessary information on how your data is handled.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold, and you may withdraw consent to marketing emails. Contact us using the email address or phone number in the footer and we will respond within a reasonable period.

6. Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies and local storage entries to remember language preference and page state. They are not used for advertising tracking. Disabling cookies in your browser may affect minor features such as form memory.

7. Updates

This policy may be revised as the site evolves. The current version takes effect from the date shown below.
