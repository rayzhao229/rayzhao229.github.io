Frequently Asked Questions

The questions buyers ask us most. If your question is not answered here, contact us and we will reply within one business day.

Which product categories do you supply?

We are a general trading company with no category restrictions. Masks and PPE (mask-grade non-woven and meltblown material, N95 and FFP3 finished masks, protective coveralls and isolation gowns, mask-making machines and meltblown lines), circuit breakers and low-voltage components, metal castings (cast iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminium), HDPE plastic products, and lifting machinery with rigging are the five key product lines we supply consistently; beyond those, we accept sourcing assignments for industrial goods in every category — electrical and mechanical parts, building materials, hardware, packaging, PPE and more. Key lines can be ordered on their own, and different categories can be consolidated into one container.

Can I order samples first?

Yes. Where material, specification or fit needs confirming, we arrange a sample shipment first. Whether the sample cost can be credited against a subsequent bulk order is negotiable and is agreed separately during commercial discussion.

Can you supply categories that are not listed on your site?

Yes. We are a general trading company and take on sourcing and supply chain assignments across all industrial goods categories — we are not restricted to the lines listed on this site. Those five are the ones we supply consistently and know best in terms of specification and inspection standards. Other categories are equally welcome: electrical and mechanical parts, building materials, hardware, packaging, PPE and more. Give us the specification, quantity and destination country, and we handle supplier search, comparison, inspection, consolidation and shipping documents.

Do you provide CE / ISO certificates and third-party inspection?

They can be arranged on request, but not every product line comes with them. In practice we send you the list of documents available for that category (material certificates, factory test reports, CE / ISO certificates where applicable) together with the quotation. Third-party inspection (SGS and similar) can be arranged on your behalf as a separately confirmed, chargeable service. Which documents ship with the goods depends on the factory and the specific order.

Can you consolidate shipments, and what quantities do you handle?

Yes. Different categories can be consolidated into the same container. We calculate the loading plan by volume and weight and show a freight split for reference. Full container load or LCL depends on the order size and destination port; below a full container we quote on an LCL basis, with the goods boxed and marked by category for easy counting and distribution.

Can you supply OEM / private label to our drawing or specification?

Yes. We take on the sourcing and supply chain assignment and route custom requirements to the factory that can make them to drawing or to sample. The normal route is: you send drawings, a sample or performance requirements, and we come back with whether the factory can meet them, the required MOQ, the sampling lead time and how material certification and inspection will be handled. Custom items need production scheduling, so the lead time is the date confirmed by the factory — we do not promise a date we cannot meet.

Can you supply finished masks (N95 / FFP3) and mask-making machines?

Yes. For finished masks we can supply N95 and FFP3 grades — we have supplied both medical and civilian grades — in flat, folded and valved styles, with OEM private label and export packing; the test reports and registration documents are listed with the quotation according to what the factory can actually provide. We also supply disposable coveralls, isolation gowns, surgical gowns and other PPE garments with material selected by application. For machinery we can supply flat mask machines, folded mask machines and meltblown production lines, configured to your target output and level of automation, including shipping, installation, commissioning and operator training. If you are setting up a local mask plant, send us your target output, mask type and go-live date and we will quote material, finished masks, equipment and PPE together.

What are your minimum order quantities?

MOQ depends on category, specification and factory scheduling. Standard protective coveralls,isolation gown,disposable gown,PPE clothing,medical gown,N95 mask,FFP3 mask,KN95 mask,finished masks,face mask manufacturer,mask making machine,mask production line,meltblown line,mask-grade non-woven fabric, HDPE fittings and circuit breakers usually accept smaller trial quantities; cast stainless parts and custom items require production scheduling and therefore higher minimums. Send us your expected quantity and we will propose a plan with the specific MOQ for that item.

Which trade and payment terms do you accept?

Common terms include EXW, FOB, CIF and DDP, agreed per order. Payment arrangements are typically discussed according to the stage and value of the order — commonly a deposit with the balance after acceptance and before shipment — subject to the terms confirmed for the order.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on category, quantity and the degree of customisation. Standard items are prepared according to stock and preparation cycles; custom items follow the factory production schedule; sea freight timing is additional and depends on the route and destination port. We state a specific lead time with every quotation — for scheduled production, the lead time follows the date confirmed by the factory.

Do you provide inspection reports and material certificates?

Yes. Standard shipments include basic shipping documents such as the packing list and shipping marks. Material certificates are available on request for stainless castings and electrical components. Third-party inspection can be arranged on your behalf as an additional service with confirmed costs. We list the document set and any charges item by item before you order.

Do you handle exports?

Our core customers are domestic industrial buyers, and we also serve purchasing parties in the Gulf region (Saudi Arabia, the UAE and others), where we can help arrange consolidated shipments and sourcing coordination. Shipping arrangement, document set and cost breakdown are explained together with the quotation — we do not make verbal commitments.
