Core Product Lines

These are the lines we supply consistently — they are not our limit. We take on sourcing orders for industrial goods in any category; send us the specification and quantity for anything else.

Castings — Cast Iron, Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Aluminium

304 / 316L castings — fittings, flanges, valve bodies and custom structural parts, made to your drawing.

Masks, PPE & Equipment

N95 / FFP3 finished masks, mask material rolls, protective coveralls and isolation gowns, plus mask machines and meltblown lines — medical and civilian grades both supplied.

Circuit Breakers & Low-Voltage Components

MCBs, RCCBs, isolators and surge protection devices; higher-class products such as air circuit breakers (ACB) can be supplied against specific projects.

HDPE Plastic Products

Pipes, fittings, temporary road plates, drainage gratings, pallets and injection-moulded parts — chemical resistant and impact resistant.

Lifting Machinery & Rigging

Electric and manual hoists, lifting accessories, wire rope and slings for plant and site work.
