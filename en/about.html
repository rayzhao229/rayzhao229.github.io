About Minghua Ruiyi

Company Profile

Beijing Minghua Ruiyi Technology Co., Ltd. is a general industrial-goods trading and sourcing company based in Beijing, backed by established upstream supply chain resources. Our key product lines are protective coveralls,isolation gown,disposable gown,PPE clothing,medical gown,N95 mask,FFP3 mask,KN95 mask,finished masks,face mask manufacturer,mask making machine,mask production line,meltblown line,mask-grade non-woven fabric, circuit breakers, metal castings (cast iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminium), HDPE plastic products and lifting machinery — but we are not limited to them: we accept sourcing and supply chain assignments for industrial goods in every category, from electrical & mechanical parts and building materials to hardware, packaging and PPE.

Industrial-goods trading for B2B buyers: one-off orders, bulk shipments and long-term framework agreements. Transparent quotations, lead times stated as they actually are.

Our team knows industrial specification systems, trade terms and inspection standards, and handles source screening, order follow-up and delivery tracking.
